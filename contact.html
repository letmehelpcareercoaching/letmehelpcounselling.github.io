Get in touch

Thinking about counselling?

If you're thinking about counselling, have a question, or would like to arrange a free initial consultation, you're welcome to get in touch.

You don't need to explain everything in your first message. A few details about what you're looking for are enough, and we can take it from there.

Send an enquiry

Please keep your message brief and avoid including sensitive or urgent personal information.

Need urgent support?

This website and enquiry form are not monitored as an emergency or crisis service.

If you need support urgently:

  • Samaritans: Call 116 123, free, day or night, 365 days a year. Visit Samaritans.
  • NHS 111: If you need urgent help for your mental health but it is not an emergency, call 111 and select the mental health option, or use NHS 111 online.
  • Shout: Text SHOUT to 85258 for free, confidential, 24/7 text support in the UK. Visit Shout.
  • Immediate danger: If you or someone else is in immediate danger, or you do not feel able to keep yourself or someone else safe, call 999 or go to A&E.
