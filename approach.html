My Approach

Counselling centred around you.

I work in a person-centred way and practise in a trauma-focused way.

Person-centred counselling

I work in a person-centred way, which means our sessions are centred around you, your experiences, your feelings and what matters to you.

I offer a space where you can talk openly, feel heard and explore your thoughts and feelings without judgement. I believe that you are the expert on your own life, and my role is to support you in developing your own understanding and finding a way forward that feels right for you.

Our conversations may focus on what you're experiencing in the here and now, or you may wish to explore how past experiences are affecting your life today. There is no set agenda or expectation about what you should talk about. We work at your pace, following what feels important to you.

Working in a trauma-focused way

I work in a trauma-focused way, having received training in this approach through Manchester Mind.

I recognise that trauma can take many forms. Some experiences are deeply distressing, frightening or life-changing, while others may involve repeated experiences of feeling unsafe, neglected, rejected or unheard.

Trauma can have a lasting impact on how we see ourselves, relate to others and experience the world around us. It can affect our sense of safety, trust, self-worth and ability to cope with everyday life.

Anxiety, depression, anger, shame, relationship difficulties and certain ways of coping can sometimes be connected to what we have experienced.

I aim to offer a space where you can explore how your experiences may be affecting you in the here and now, whether through your feelings, relationships, sense of self or everyday life. What matters to you guides our work together, at your own pace.

Looking beyond labels and behaviour

Throughout my professional life, including my work with young people and young adults and my four years with Nacro, I have worked with people whose circumstances, behaviour or past choices could easily become the thing others used to define them.

I don't believe that is the whole person.

If you have offended in the past, are at risk of offending, have become caught in patterns or relationships you want to change, or simply feel that your past continues to follow you, counselling can offer somewhere to explore what has brought you to this point and what you want your future to look like.

My role isn't to judge your past or tell you how to live your life. It is to understand you, including the experiences that have shaped you, and work alongside you as you decide what moving forward means.

We start with you

You don't need to arrive knowing exactly what you want to talk about. You may have one particular issue, several things that seem connected, or simply a feeling that something isn't right.

We start wherever you are.
